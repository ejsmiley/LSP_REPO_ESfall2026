# LSP Course Orientation
## Course policy questions

### Are late programming assignments accepted?
No, late assignments accepted.

### Where will official course announcements be posted?
Piazza.

### How will examination dates be announced?
Announced in class and posted on canvas 2 weeks in advance.

### Under what circumstances will a makeup examination be considered?
Only under extraordinary conditions outside of the students control

### Is AI permitted in this course?
If permitted by the professor.

### Which course policy will have the greatest impact on your success this semester? Explain why (2-3 sentences)
Attendance and participation will have the greatest impact because they are the foundation for everything else in the class. Without proper guidance, I can fall behind and impact my grade negatively.

### When are office hours for LSP Fall 2026?
Office hours are by Zoom appointment.

### What is your responsibility for monitoring Piazza, and are you responsible for assignment updates, clarifications, or deadline changes posted there?
I am responsible for checking Piazza daily for all course-related updates, clarifications, or deadline changes and other announcements posted there?

### What documentation is required if you are unable to take an examination because of a medical issue or other approved circumstance?
Enough to disclose student was unable to complete the examination.

### Does scholarship status entitle a student to different grading, deadline, attendance, or course requirements?
No, scholarship status doesn't entitle students to any special treatment regarding grading, deadlines, attendance, or course requirements. All students are expected to meet the same course standards and deadlines.

### Acknowledgement
"I acknowledge that I have read the course syllabus and understand that I am responsible for complying with all course policies, assignment requirements, and monitoring Piazza throughout the semester"
